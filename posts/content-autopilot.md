# Content Autopilot

**An AI content pipeline: draft, review, approve, and publish on a schedule.**

Content Autopilot generates draft posts, routes them through a human review step, and publishes approved content automatically. It's a clean example of human-in-the-loop automation: the machine does the volume, the human keeps the judgment.

## Highlights
- AI-drafted posts with review/edit/redo controls
- One-click publish to social scheduling (Buffer)
- Built for wearable/AR review surfaces (Meta Ray-Ban, Quest)

## Stack
Node.js, LLM drafting, Buffer API
