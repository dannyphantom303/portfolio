# Obsidian Brain — Knowledge Graph Dashboard

**A live 3D visualization of a personal knowledge vault.**

Obsidian Brain renders a personal knowledge base as an interactive 3D graph, regenerating from the vault so it's always current. It's a study in turning unstructured notes into navigable structure — the same skill behind the finance tracking systems.

## Highlights
- Live regeneration from source notes
- Interactive 3D graph navigation
- Search + drill-down into connected ideas

## Stack
Python, Node.js, WebGL
