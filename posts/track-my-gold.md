# Track My Gold

**A YouTube engagement + affiliate dashboard for a gold-prospecting content channel.**

Track My Gold is a content-operation dashboard: it surfaces video performance, manages an approval queue for comments and engagement, tracks affiliate revenue, and runs SEO checks — all in one panel. It shows how I turn a creator workflow into a measurable, automatable pipeline rather than a pile of spreadsheets.

## Highlights
- Unified metrics across YouTube, Instagram, and affiliate channels
- Approval queue for outbound engagement (review, edit, approve)
- SEO + content-strategy panels driving weekly decisions

## Stack
Node.js, YouTube API, Instagram API, SEO tooling
