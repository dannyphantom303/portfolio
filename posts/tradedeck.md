# TradeDeck

**Multi-agent sports & market desk.**

TradeDeck is one of the systems in my AI portfolio. Multi-agent sports & market desk. A team of specialist agents (scout, news, quant, risk, execution, journal) share one server-side model router that picks the best installed model for each task and records which model handled it and why — no agent hard-codes its own model. Live charts, screener, signals, macro, portfolio, risk, backtest, alerts, journal, jobs and approvals.
