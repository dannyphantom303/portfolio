# Peril Adventure Wear

**Content autopilot for the shirt line — the same approve-first flow as Track My Gold, but for @peril_adventures.**

Peril Adventure Wear is one of the systems in my AI portfolio. Content autopilot for the shirt line — the same approve-first flow as Track My Gold, but for @peril_adventures. Drafts alternate product shots, trail ethos and camp scenes across three layouts and six print palettes, in a minimal vintage outdoor-brand look: condensed uppercase headlines, tracked small caps, topographic contour lines and low-contrast ridges, cream/forest/rust paper. Copy is generated against verified brand facts only — no invented prices or claims — and the images are drawn locally, never diffusion-generated. Review, edit, approve, publish to Buffer.
