## How it works

- **Landing and capture** — A public consulting page with the services, a calculator and an email capture — the entry point for inbound work.

- **Tiers and templates** — Packaged service tiers plus proposal templates, so a serious enquiry costs minutes to answer, not an afternoon.

- **Intake** — A brief is captured as structured fields: scope, deadline, budget, access required.

- **Delivery on the same board** — Client work moves through the same task bus as engineering work, so commitments and capacity are visible in one place.

- **Accounts** — Client records and deliverables live next to the cards that produce them.

## What it costs
The tooling cost is effectively zero — free-tier models and a local board. The cost that matters is your own time, which is exactly why the proposals are templated and the intake is structured.

## How to build it this way

- **One board for both kinds of work** — Client commitments and product work in the same queue, so over-commitment is visible early.

- **Template the paperwork** — Proposals, scopes and hand-off notes should be assembled, not written from scratch.

- **Charge for outcomes** — Products pay later, consulting pays now; keep both, and price the one that pays now properly.

## What I learned

- The same automation that runs a business can run the business that sells the automation.

- Structured intake removes most of the back-and-forth that makes small projects unprofitable.
