# Sport — Multi-Agent AI Operations System

**A Chief-of-Staff AI that coordinates venture CEOs and specialist agents across every project.**

Sport is the operating system for a portfolio of AI ventures. It runs a hierarchy of agents — an executive coordinator, venture CEOs, and specialists — over a shared kanban/task bus, with approval gates on anything that leaves the machine. Built to remove the human bottleneck from repetitive operations: monitoring, research, drafting, and reporting all run as scheduled agent jobs.

## Highlights
- Multi-agent orchestration with role hierarchy and task routing
- Approval-gated outbound actions (nothing posts or sends without sign-off)
- Scheduled autonomous jobs + realtime status dashboard

## Stack
Hermes Agent, Node.js, SQLite, Tailscale
