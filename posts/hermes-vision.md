# Hermes Vision — AI on Phone & Glasses

**A mobile/wearable client that connects a device to a running AI agent session.**

Hermes Vision puts a live AI agent session on your phone or smart glasses: pick up a session, choose a model, and stream replies in real time. The interesting engineering is the realtime streaming layer and the wearable-first UI (grid navigation, focus modes, wheel/swipe input).

## Highlights
- Server-sent-event streaming of agent replies
- Wearable-first input model (2-D grid nav, focus paging)
- Installable PWA with offline app shell

## Stack
PWA, SSE streaming, Service workers
