# Film Room

**Short-film production desk.**

Film Room is one of the systems in my AI portfolio. Short-film production desk. Type an idea, set a budget, and it prices the film against a live Higgsfield rate card measured straight from the API — every model's real $/second, cost-versus-quality trade, and how many minutes of finished picture your money actually buys. The project board tracks every shot: the still that becomes its first frame, whether the clip is rendered, per-shot cost and total spend against the ledger. Chat panel talks to the agent that owns the project, so it can rewrite a prompt, regenerate a frame or start a render from here.
